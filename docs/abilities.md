# Abilities

Every ability below ships in the default skill tree. All numbers are defaults from
`config/remnants/skills_and_powers/abilities.json` and can be changed per server.
Damage and healing grow with your tree stats, and with your RPG level when Remnants | RPG Player Skills is installed.


## Pyromancy (Fire)

| Ability | What it does | Cost | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ember Bolt**<br>`ember_bolt` | Hurl a searing bolt that deals 6 Fire damage and ignites the target for 4s. | 10 Essence | 1.5s |
| **Explosive Orb**<br>`explosive_orb` | Launch a volatile orb that bursts for 10 Fire damage in a 3.5-block radius. It never breaks blocks. | 25 Essence | 6s |
| **Ember Shield**<br>`ember_shield` | Wreathe yourself in embers for 10s: gain 8 absorption Health, and attackers burn for 3s. | 30 Essence | 18s |
| **Inferno Wave**<br>`inferno_wave` | A ring of flame rolls out 7 blocks, dealing 8 Fire damage and igniting enemies for 4s. | 40 Essence | 12s |
| **Meteor** (ultimate)<br>`meteor` | Call a meteor onto the spot you aim at within 32 blocks. After 1.2s it strikes for 22 Fire damage in a 5-block radius. | 60 Essence | 30s |
