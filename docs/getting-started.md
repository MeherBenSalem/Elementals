# Getting started

## Open the tree

Press **O** to open the Remnant Constellation. The tree has 522 nodes in six schools around a gold
inner ring:

| School | Element | Plays like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pyromancy | Fire | Burning damage, explosions, glass-cannon builds |
| Stormcalling | Storm | Chains, crits, mobility |
| Umbral Arts | Void | Leech, curses, assassination |
| Tidecalling | Frost | Control, Essence, sustain |
| Earthshaping | Earth | Armor, knockback, area denial |
| Verdance | Life | Healing, support, thorns |

## Choose an origin

Click any of the six star-shaped origin nodes on the inner ring. That stages your first node. Press
**Enter** (or **Apply**) to confirm. Eating a **Fire Seed** or **Water Seed** grants the Pyromancy or
Tidecalling origin for free.

## Spend points

- **Click** any node: the shortest path to it is staged in green, and the tooltip shows its cost.
- **Right-click** an allocated node to mark it for refund (red). Refunds cost XP levels; see the config.
- **Shift-click** to add nodes to a **plan** (blue). Plans spend nothing. **Use Plan** stages as much of
  the plan as you can afford.
- **Enter** or **Apply** confirms everything at once. The server checks the whole build before it
  changes anything. **Undo** discards staged changes.
- **Ctrl+F** searches names, stats and descriptions. **Enter** jumps to the next match.
- **Drag** to pan, **scroll** to zoom at the cursor, **H** to return to your build, **S** for the stat
  summary. Click the minimap to jump.

## Abilities

Ability nodes (diamond frames) unlock active powers. New abilities fill empty slots automatically. To
choose, click a slot at the bottom of the tree screen, or hover an unlocked ability node and press
**1** to **4**.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| R | Cast slot 1 |
| G | Cast slot 2 |
| Z | Cast slot 3 |
| H | Cast slot 4 |

All keys can be rebound under **Controls → Remnants | Skills and Powers**. Abilities cost **Essence**
(the blue bar above the ability bar), which refills over time.

## Earning points

Without RPG Player Skills, you gain **Power XP** by killing mobs (more from tougher mobs) and by casting.
Each Power level gives one skill point. With [RPG Player Skills](rpg-player-skills.md) installed, your
RPG level gives the points instead.

**Remnant Tomes** grant extra points. Each player can read up to 20 by default.
