# Keystones

Keystones are the large nodes at the outer edge of each school. Each one changes a rule of
the game, usually with a cost. Custom trees refer to them by id.

| Keystone | Id | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pyre Heart** | `pyre_heart` | You take no damage from burning. Your ignites last twice as long. |
| **Glass Cannon** | `glass_cannon` | +40% Ability Damage. 25% less Max Health. |
| **Conduit** | `conduit` | Every 4th ability you cast costs no Essence and deals double damage. |
| **Windborne** | `windborne` | You take no fall damage. +15% Movement Speed. 15% less Armor. |
| **Blood Pact** | `blood_pact` | Abilities cost Health instead of Essence: 1 Health per 10 Essence. +25% Ability Damage. |
| **Soul Eater** | `soul_eater` | Killing an enemy restores 15% of your Max Essence and 2 Health. |
| **Eternal Tide** | `eternal_tide` | Essence regenerates twice as fast. 30% less Max Essence. |
| **Glacial Bulwark** | `glacial_bulwark` | Take 20% less damage while above half Health. Enemies that hit you are chilled. |
| **Unyielding** | `unyielding` | You cannot be knocked back. +30% Armor. 10% less Movement Speed. |
| **Bedrock Will** | `bedrock_will` | Take 15% less damage. Ability cooldowns are 20% longer. |
| **Undying Grove** | `undying_grove` | Once every 5 minutes, a killing blow instead leaves you at 30% Health and roots nearby enemies. |
| **Verdant Bloom** | `verdant_bloom` | Your healing is 50% stronger. Regenerate 0.5 Health per second. 20% less Ability Damage. |
