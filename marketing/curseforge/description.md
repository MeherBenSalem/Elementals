## A skill tree you can get lost in

**Remnants | Skills and Powers** gives every player a 522-node passive skill tree in six schools of magic, 30 castable abilities and 12 keystones that bend the rules. Plan a path before you spend a point, share your build as a one-line code, then cast from a hotbar of four powers. It works on its own, and links up with **[Remnants | RPG Player Skills](https://modrinth.com/mod/remnants-rpg-stats-skills-progression)** when both are installed.

**2.0 "The Remnant Constellation"** is a full rework of *Elementals*. Same mod id, so 1.x worlds and players carry over.

- **522 nodes** around a shared central ring: 54 notables, 30 abilities, 12 keystones and hundreds of minor nodes.
- **Notable wheels:** each branch passes through rings of six themed nodes around a named notable.
- **Hybrid bridges** join neighbouring schools, with notables that mix both elements: Plasma Arc, Ionized Flame, Black Ice, Permafrost, Overgrowth and more.
- **No school is locked.** Neighbouring schools are only three nodes apart, so a Pyromancer can reach Stormcalling's keystones.
- **32 stats:** vanilla attributes (health, armor, melee damage, speed...) plus ability damage, per-element damage, crit, cooldown reduction, area, duration, lifesteal, Essence, damage reduction, evasion, thorns and more. Vanilla stats are real attribute modifiers, so other mods see them.

![Six Schools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-schools.png)

Your first point picks an origin: one of the six glowing start nodes on the inner ring. From there, path anywhere.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pyromancy** | Fire | Ember Bolt, Explosive Orb, Ember Shield, Inferno Wave, **Meteor** |
| **Stormcalling** | Storm | Chain Lightning, Static Field, Thunderclap, Gale Step, **Tempest** |
| **Umbral Arts** | Void | Shadow Bolt, Soul Harvest, Void Rift, Shadow Step, **Eclipse** |
| **Tidecalling** | Frost | Frost Lance, Tidebinder, Glacial Prison, Tidal Surge, **Maelstrom** |
| **Earthshaping** | Earth | Stone Spear, Stone Skin, Tremor, Fissure, **Mountain's Wrath** |
| **Verdance** | Life | Mending Light, Thorn Burst, Sanctuary, Radiant Nova, **Verdant Rebirth** |

Ultimates (in bold) sit behind each school's Mastery notable and cost 2 points.

![Abilities](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-abilities.png)

![Spells that land hard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-abilities.png)

![Casting Tempest, Chain Lightning, Meteor and Inferno Wave](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/gifs/casting.gif)

- **Four cast slots** on **R, Z, B and H** (rebindable). New abilities fill empty slots on their own. To choose, click a slot in the tree screen or hover an ability node and press 1 to 4.
- Abilities cost **Essence**, which refills over time. Your tree raises their damage, crit chance, area, duration and Essence, and lowers their cooldowns.
- Projectiles, beams, zones, dashes, heals, shields and summons from the sky, all drawn with vanilla particles: **no resource pack** and nothing extra for clients to download.
- **Safe by default:** abilities never break blocks, never hit teammates or your pets, and leave other players alone unless the server turns PvP on.
- Every cost, cooldown, damage value, radius and duration is in `abilities.json`, and any ability can be disabled.

![In Combat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-combat.png)

![Cast from the hotbar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-combat.png)

- The **ability bar** sits right of the hotbar with cooldown sweeps and the key for each slot. A blue tint means you are short on Essence.
- The **Essence bar** and your Power XP sit just above it, with a reminder when you have skill points to spend.
- Level-up banners and notices appear at the top of the screen.
- Hidden with F1, moves aside for a left-handed offhand slot, and stays clear of the Remnants | RPG Player Skills HUD.

![Planning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-planning.png)

![See the path. Then commit.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-planning.png)

Press **I** to open the tree.

- **Hover any node** to light up the cheapest path to it, with its total cost and every stat it grants.
- **Click** to stage that path. **Right-click** to stage a refund. Nothing changes until you press **Apply** (or Enter), and **Undo** throws the staged changes away. The server applies the whole build at once, or nothing.
- The **Stats** panel (S) totals your build and shows **current → after** for every staged change.
- **Shift-click** to add nodes to a plan you cannot afford yet. **Use Plan** later stages as much as you can pay for.
- **Search** (Ctrl+F) any node, stat or keystone and fly straight to it. A **minimap** jumps anywhere, and the screen fits every GUI scale.

![Build Codes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-share.png)

![Share a build in one line](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-share.png)

- **Share** copies your build as a short code such as `RSP1-BWJpfwwAEgEBAQEBAQEEAQE`. Post it in chat, a wiki or a guide.
- Paste a code to import it as a **plan**. Imports never spend points.
- `/powers build` prints a clickable code for yourself, or for any player if you are an operator.

![RPG Player Skills](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-rpg.png)

![Better together](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-rpg.png)

Install **Remnants | RPG Player Skills** next to Skills and Powers and they link up on their own. Neither mod requires the other.

| | Skills and Powers alone | With RPG Player Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill points | From Power levels | From RPG levels: 1 point per RPG level |
| Level in the tree screen | Power Level and XP bar | RPG Level |
| Ability damage | Your tree | Your tree + 0.5% per RPG level |
| Node requirements | Power level | RPG level too |

- **Retroactive:** a player who is already RPG level 30 starts with their 30 points.
- RPG attribute points and tree nodes stack, since both use vanilla attribute modifiers.
- Default keys never overlap: RPG Player Skills uses **K**, Skills and Powers uses **I** and **R, Z, B, H**.
- Choose how they combine with `rpg_player_skills.mode`: `auto`, `replace`, `add` or `off`.

![Progression](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-progression.png)

![Earn it your way](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-progression.png)

- **Power XP** from killing mobs (more for tougher mobs, triple for bosses) and from casting. Each Power level is a skill point, up to level 100.
- **Remnant Tome:** +1 skill point, up to 20 per player. A rare Fire Golem drop, also craftable from an Amethyst Shard, a Diamond, a Book, a Fire Rune and a Water Rune.
- **Respec at any time.** Refund single nodes for 1 XP level each, or press **Reset Tree** for 10 levels. A **Tome of Unbinding** (Book, Ender Pearl and 2 Lapis) resets the whole tree for free.
- **Fire Golems** roam the Nether Wastes, Crimson Forests and Basalt Deltas, dropping Fire Runes and Magma Cream.

![Server Owners](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-config.png)

![Your tree, your rules](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/card-config.png)

- **The server owns the tree.** It sends the tree, ability numbers and settings to every client on join, so players never need a config.
- **Write your own tree** in `skill_tree.json`: positions, links, icons, stats, costs and flavor text. Run `/powers reload` and every online player gets it, with invalid allocations refunded and explained.
- **Node requirements and hooks:** require a Power or RPG level, and run commands when a node is allocated or refunded.
- **Admin commands:** `/powers` (or `/skills`) for info, build codes, points, levels, XP, resets, origins, granting nodes, clearing cooldowns, casting any ability and reloading.
- Player data is plain JSON in `<world>/remnants_powers/`, written safely.

![Everything Else](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-everything.png)

- **Upgrading from Elementals 1.x:** players who chose Fire or Water get the Pyromancy or Tidecalling origin for free, plus 3 bonus points per old elemental level. Old spell cooldowns move into `abilities.json`.
- **Spell books** still cast Explosive Orb, Ember Shield and Inferno Wave for Pyromancy players who reach each book's level.
- **Fire and Water Runes**, element seeds and the Fire Golem spawn egg are in the creative tab.
- No library dependencies. Fabric only needs Fabric API.

- Optional: **Remnants | RPG Player Skills** on the server and clients.
- Updating keeps your configuration values and adds any new options.

![Questions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/main/marketing/images/header-faq.png)

**Do I need Remnants | RPG Player Skills?**
No. On its own, Skills and Powers gives points from Power levels. With RPG Player Skills installed, RPG levels give the points instead (or as well, with `mode: add`).

**Will my Elementals 1.x world break?**
No. The mod id is still `elementals`, so items, Fire Golems and worlds load as before, and players are migrated on their first join.

**A key does nothing, or does two things.**
Defaults are I, R, Z, B and H, chosen because vanilla binds none of them on any supported version. Rebind them under **Controls → Remnants | Skills and Powers**.

**Can abilities grief or hurt other players?**
They never break blocks. They ignore teammates and your pets, and only hit players if the server sets `abilities_hurt_players` to `true`.

**Can I make my own tree?**
Yes. Edit `skill_tree.json`, set `use_custom_tree` to `true` and run `/powers reload`. A copy of the built-in tree is written to `skill_tree.default.json` for reference.
