# Remnants | Skills and Powers 2.0.0

Elementals is now **Remnants | Skills and Powers**: a full passive skill tree with active abilities,
built to work alone or alongside **Remnants | RPG Player Skills**.

Supported: **1.20.1** (Fabric, Forge), **1.21.1** (Fabric, NeoForge), **26.2** (Fabric, NeoForge)
and **26.3** (Fabric, NeoForge, new).

## The Remnant Constellation

- A 522-node passive tree in six schools: Pyromancy, Stormcalling, Umbral Arts, Tidecalling,
  Earthshaping and Verdance. Pick any origin, then path anywhere: neighbouring schools are three
  nodes apart and hybrid bridges (Plasma Arc, Black Ice, Phoenix Ash...) reward mixing.
- 54 notables, 30 abilities and 12 keystones that bend the rules (Glass Cannon, Conduit, Blood Pact,
  Soul Eater, Eternal Tide, Undying Grove...).
- 32 stats: vanilla attributes plus ability power, per-element damage, crit, cooldown reduction,
  area, duration, lifesteal, Essence, damage reduction, dodge, thorns, burn and chill on hit.

## The tree screen (press I)

- Hover any node to see the full path, its point cost and the stats you would gain.
- Click to stage a path, right-click to refund, then **Apply** (Enter) or **Undo**. The server applies
  the whole build at once or nothing.
- **Plan mode** (shift-click), **search** with fly-to (Ctrl+F), a clickable **minimap**, zoom, and a
  **Stats** panel that shows current → after for every staged change.
- **Build codes**: share a build as `RSP1-...` text and import someone else's as a plan.
- Layout adapts to every GUI scale, down to 720p on Auto.

## Abilities

- 30 abilities, five per school, including a two-point ultimate (Meteor, Tempest, Eclipse...).
- Four cast slots on **R / Z / B / H** (rebindable). New abilities fill empty slots automatically;
  click a slot in the tree screen or press 1-4 over an ability node to choose.
- Abilities cost **Essence** (blue bar), scale with your tree, never break blocks, respect teams and
  are PvP-safe by default.
- New HUD: ability bar right of the hotbar with cooldown sweeps, Essence and XP bars, level-up
  banners and a skill point reminder.

## Progression

- Power levels from killing mobs (scaled by their health, bosses ×3) and casting.
- **Remnant Tome** (rare Fire Golem drop, craftable): +1 skill point, up to 20 per player.
- Respec freely: refund single nodes (1 XP level each by default) or use **Reset Tree** (10 levels).
  A **Tome of Unbinding** resets the whole tree for free.
- Every number is in `config/remnants/skills_and_powers/general.json` and `abilities.json`.

## Remnants | RPG Player Skills integration

- Detected automatically. RPG levels grant skill points (1 per level by default, retroactive), the
  tree shows your RPG level, and abilities gain +0.5% damage per RPG level.
- Modes: `auto`, `replace`, `add`, `off`. Custom nodes can require an RPG level or run RPG Player
  Skills commands when allocated.
- No key or HUD overlap between the two mods.

## For server owners

- The tree lives on the server: edit `skill_tree.json`, run `/powers reload`, and every client gets
  the new tree. Invalid allocations are refunded with a message.
- `/powers` (alias `/skills`): info, build, points, level, xp, reset, origin, grant, cooldowns,
  cast, reload.
- Nodes can require a Power or RPG level and run commands on allocate or refund.
- Player data is plain JSON in `<world>/remnants_powers/`, written atomically.

## Upgrading from Elementals 1.x

- Same mod id: worlds, items and the Fire Golem carry over.
- Players who chose Fire or Water get the Pyromancy or Tidecalling origin free, plus 3 bonus points
  per old elemental level. Old spell cooldowns are copied into `abilities.json`.

## Other changes

- The Fire Golem is now an iron-golem-sized nether brute with its own texture. It spawns in Nether
  Wastes, Crimson Forests and Basalt Deltas and drops Fire Runes, Magma Cream and, rarely, a Remnant Tome.
- Spell books still cast their ability for Pyromancy players who reach the book's level, now using
  Essence and the same cooldowns as the tree.
- Progression is server-authoritative and resynced on join, respawn, dimension change and reload.
