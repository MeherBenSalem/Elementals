# Remnants | Skills and Powers 2.0.1

A bug-fix release for the tree screen on Minecraft 26.3.

## Fixes

- **26.3 (Fabric and NeoForge): left click now allocates nodes in the skill tree.** Minecraft 26.3
  numbers mouse buttons differently (left is 1, right is 3), so the tree screen read every left
  click as a right click and ignored right clicks. Clicking nodes, the Apply/Undo/Stats/Share
  buttons and the ability slots works again, and right click refunds as intended.
  ([#1](https://github.com/MeherBenSalem/Elementals/issues/1))

1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 26.2 are unchanged apart from the version number.
